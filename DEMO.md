# Detour: three-minute demonstration

All content shown is fictional. Reset before presenting, then avoid running tests
or a second reset while judges are using the app.

1. **Find your interests (30 seconds).** Open the attendee view. Search for
   `sniffing interpod conversations` with Meaning only. Explain the top networking
   matches. Switch to Keywords only to show the difference, then back to hybrid.
   Choose Kubernetes/networking interests and any relevant demo accessibility filter.
2. **Build the day (20 seconds).** Clear the search and save Inside Cilium's Datapath
   at 9 AM and Kernel Superpowers for Cluster Traffic at 11 AM Mountain Time.
   Show that overlapping sessions are rejected and times can display in UTC.
3. **A change of plans (25 seconds).** In a second tab, open Organizer. Mark
   Inside Cilium's Datapath full. Return to the attendee tab; the next poll detects
   the change. Click Find my detour.
4. **Find another path (35 seconds).** Show the original disruption and replacement
   explanations: shared topics, compatible time, transfer time and verified demo
   accessibility filters. Accept one. The new session appears in the persisted agenda.
   If preferences leave no valid alternative, show the explicit no-alternative message.
5. **Follow the evidence (35 seconds).** Open the corresponding trace in Dash0.
   Show the request, semantic search, disruption detection, ranking and replanning
   spans, plus latency and outcome metrics. Present the actual investigation recorded
   in STORY.md; never claim an unverified integration works.
6. **Show how it shipped (35 seconds).** Open Radar's bumblebee Deployment, Timeline,
   Checks and hosted investigation. Show the built SHA matching `/healthz`. Open
   Gitar-reviewed PRs, the concrete issues it caught and the verified fixes/CI result.

The organizer controls, room occupancy, speaker names, transfer times and accessibility
metadata are synthetic demonstration features. Detour does not monitor real rooms.
